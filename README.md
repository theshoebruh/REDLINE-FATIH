## The idea

Two meanings in one name: *redline* is the limit an engine is pushed to — speed, RPM,
car culture. *Faith* is the Christian foundation. Neither side is an afterthought and
neither is watered down.

Collections map to car scenes rather than one narrow lane: JDM, GDM, Street,
Motorsport, Youth. A rotary owner, a Porsche owner, a track-day regular and a kid with
his first hatch should all feel this was made for them.

## What makes it not just another shop

The **custom print system**. A customer uploads their own artwork, sizes it on the
shirt, and sees the price move live — priced on the visible printed area, not file
size. Behind that, orders are auto-packed onto shared 100 × 60 cm supplier sheets like
Tetris, with new sheets opened as they fill. The customer experience stays four steps:
*choose shirt → upload artwork → resize → order.* The complexity stays hidden.
